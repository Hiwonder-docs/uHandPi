**2.2.2 Color Classification**

:::{Note}
- Please perform this game in a well-lit indoor environment, but try to avoid strong direct sunlight. 
- During recognition, please ensure that there are no objects with similar or identical colors to the target within the camera's field of view to avoid interference with recognition.
- If the recognition effect is unsatisfactory, you can go to the directory "[Quick User Experience\3.Adjust Color Threshold]()" to learn how to adjust the color threshold.
:::

(1) Click “Color Classification” icon to proceed to the game interface. This interface is divided into three parts:

<img src="../_static/media/2.quick_user_experience/2.1/image19.png" class="common_img" />

① The area at the top of the interface is the status bar.

② On the left side of the interface is the area for activating, deactivating, and selecting colors for the game.

③ On the right side of the interface is the area for the camera feedback screen.


(2) Click “Start Classification”, check the classify colors, and place the corresponding colored balls individually in front of the camera. Take red as an example, when red is detected, the identified red will be highlighted in the feedback image. The RGB lights on the expansion board will illuminate the corresponding color, place the ball in the center of the hand, the hand will grasp the ball, and then place the ball in the corresponding position.

<img src="../_static/media/2.quick_user_experience/2.1/image19.png" class="common_img" />

| Identified Color | Identified Result                                            |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Red              | RGB light is red, place the red ball to the left side of the hand |
| Blue             | RGB light is blue, place the red ball to the right side of the hand |

If you want to return the game selection interface, you can click a blank area to bring up the status bar. Then click the icon<img src="../_static/media/2.quick_user_experience/2.1/image17.png"  />on the left.
